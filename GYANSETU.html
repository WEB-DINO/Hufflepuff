GYAN SETU "Bridge To Education"

A transformative, humanitarian project designed to dismantle the educational barriers facing India's most vulnerable—**homeless and uneducated children**.

How It Works

Gyan Setu is a reliable and standardized digital school system. We've designed a complete solution to provide accessible, guided, and validated learning for every child.

Universal Access and Smart Design

The platform’s design is centered on universal accessibility. Every child receives a personalized login key that instantly generates a custom learning path, ensuring their studies are strictly aligned with the official NCERT syllabus for their precise educational stage.

  • ✔

    Provides a standardized, high-quality curriculum for every student.

Strategic Deployment and Guided Learning

The learning journey is reinforced 24 hours a day by an advanced AI Chatbot. It acts as a patient, guided tutor, fostering true understanding by clarifying concepts and providing hints without ever giving away final answers.

Hi! I see you're working on fractions. What part is tricky?
I don't get how to add 1/2 and 1/4.
Great question! To add them, they need the same "bottom number" (denominator). Can you think of a number that both 2 and 4 go into?

Assessment and Validation

To cement knowledge and build essential test-taking confidence, the platform features a rigorous assessment structure that mirrors a traditional academic calendar. This validation empowers students and proves the system's effectiveness.

Scalable & Strategic Deployment

Gyan Setu is designed for scalable impact. The robust terminals can be deployed strategically in public places, maximizing reach and visibility to children who may not have access to stationary schooling.

Our Vision:

To transform any public space—a bus stand, a community center, a railway station—into a safe, reliable, and effective learning hub for children in need.

Our Ultimate Goal

This comprehensive structure is not just about teaching subjects; it's about restoring dignity and opening clear pathways to opportunity.

To be a decisive tool in the national mission to significantly increase the literacy rate.
